01 OUR SCHOOL

Pendidikan usia dini yang berakar pada nilaiEarly education rooted in values

Membentuk karakter,
bukan hanya pengetahuan.Building character,
not only knowledge.

BUNAYYA memadukan Kurikulum Merdeka dengan pendidikan agama Islam melalui sistem belajar sentra untuk membentuk karakter sholeh, mandiri, cerdas, terampil, dan bertanggung jawab.BUNAYYA combines the Merdeka Curriculum with Islamic education through centre-based learning to nurture faith, independence, intelligence, skills, and responsibility.

“Menjadi sekolah unggul harapan ummat dalam mendidik siswa cinta Al Quran, cerdas berprestasi, berakhlak mulia, sebagai pemimpin masa depan berwawasan kebangsaan.”“To become an excellent school for the ummah, educating students who love the Qur’an, achieve with intelligence, show noble character, and become future leaders with a national outlook.”Visi BUNAYYABUNAYYA Vision
Baca misi sekolahRead the school mission
  1. Mendidik siswa cerdas berprestasi berakhlak mulia sebagai pemimpin masa depan yang berwawasan kebangsaan.Educate intelligent, high-achieving students of noble character as future leaders with a national outlook.
  2. Mewujudkan pilar keunggulan Sekolah Islam Terpadu Bunayya.Realise the pillars of excellence of Bunayya Integrated Islamic School.
  3. Mengembangkan Sekolah Islam Terpadu Bunayya sebagai pusat pembelajaran.Develop Bunayya Integrated Islamic School as a centre of learning.

01

Agama Islam & Al-Qur’anIslam & the Qur’an

Tahsin metode Yanbu’a, hafalan surat pendek Juz 30, hadits dan doa sehari-hari, siroh nabi dan rasul, Asmaul Husna, dan praktik ibadah.Yanbu’a tahsin, short surah memorisation from Juz 30, hadith and daily prayers, stories of prophets, Asmaul Husna, and worship practice.

02

Kemampuan dasarFoundational skills

Sosial-emosi, bahasa, kognitif—sains dan matematika—serta motorik kasar dan halus.Social-emotional, language, cognitive—science and mathematics—and gross and fine motor development.

03

Materi penunjangSupporting subjects

Bahasa Arab dan Inggris, olahraga (senam irama), dan pembiasaan menabung.Arabic and English, rhythmic exercise, and the habit of saving.

Apakah simulasi biaya sudah termasuk biaya bulanan?Does the estimate include monthly fees?
